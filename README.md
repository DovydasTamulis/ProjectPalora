🌟 What is AI Pal/Friend?
AI Pal/Friend is more than just an app—it's your personalized digital companion designed to help you stay focused, motivated, and entertained throughout your day. With its unique characters, gamified leveling system, and AI-powered features, it’s like having a best friend in your pocket! 💬💡

🎯 Key Features
1. Focus Assistant
   Pomodoro Timer: Stay productive with customizable work/break cycles.
   Task Management: Add, track, and complete tasks effortlessly.
   Reminders: Never miss a deadline with timely notifications.
2. Friend
   Conversational AI: Chat with your AI Pal anytime—ask questions, share thoughts, or just vent!
   Mood Booster: Get jokes, affirmations, and uplifting messages when you need them most.
3. Interests Finder
   Fun Facts & Trivia: Learn something new every day with quirky facts and trivia games.
   Curiosity Mode: Explore niche topics or ask random questions.
4. Motivator
   Goal Tracking: Set personal goals and track your progress.
   Motivational Messages: Get encouragement during goal reminders.
5. Teacher
   Educational Content: Bite-sized lessons, tutorials, and explanations on various topics.
   Learning Modules: Quizzes and challenges to keep your brain sharp.
6. Character System
   Three Unique Characters: Choose from Friendly Buddy, Task Master, or Curious Explorer.
   Leveling System: Unlock new abilities and content as your character levels up!
